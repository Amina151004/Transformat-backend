1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect and store, via our authentication provider (Supabase):

  • Your email address
  • Your password (stored as a secure hash — we never see or store it in plain text)
  • Account creation date and authentication metadata

1.2 Files You Upload

Files you upload for conversion (PDF, DOCX, PPTX, PNG, JPEG) are processed temporarily on our servers and are automatically deleted after conversion completes or after a short retention window, whichever comes first. We do not read, review, or use the content of your files for any purpose other than performing the conversion you requested.

1.3 Usage Data

We track conversion counts and usage limits (e.g., how many conversions you've used this month) to enforce Free and Pro tier limits. This is stored against your account and resets monthly.

1.4 Billing Information

If you subscribe to Transformat Pro, payment is processed entirely by Stripe. We do not collect or store your credit card number, CVC, or full billing address. We receive and store only:

  • Your Stripe customer ID
  • Subscription status (active, canceled, past due, etc.)
  • Billing period dates

1.5 Device & Technical Data

Standard technical data such as app version, device type, and error logs may be collected to diagnose crashes and improve reliability.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the file conversion service
  • To authenticate you and maintain your account
  • To enforce Free tier limits and manage Pro subscriptions
  • To process payments and manage billing via Stripe
  • To send essential account-related emails (password reset, billing issues)
  • To diagnose bugs and improve app performance and reliability

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your uploaded files for advertising, training, or any purpose beyond conversion.
